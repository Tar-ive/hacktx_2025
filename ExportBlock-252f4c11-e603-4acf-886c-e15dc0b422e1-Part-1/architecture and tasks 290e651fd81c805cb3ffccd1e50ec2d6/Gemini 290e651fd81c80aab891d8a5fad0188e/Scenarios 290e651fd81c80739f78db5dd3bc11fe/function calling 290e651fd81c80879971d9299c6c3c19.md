**Function Calling Workflow:**

**Scenario: User asks: "Create a budget for groceries that's 10% less than what I've been spending"**

**Step 1: Gemini Receives Request**

- Intent: budget_creation
- Required data: Current grocery spending
- Action: Need to calculate 10% reduction

**Step 2: Gemini Plans Execution**

- Gemini generates internal plan:
    1. Call search_transactions to get last 3 months of grocery spending
    2. Calculate average monthly spending
    3. Reduce by 10%
    4. Call create_budget with new amount
    5. Respond to user with confirmation

**Step 3: First Function Call**

- Gemini calls: search_transactions({
filters: {
category: "Groceries",
start_date: "2025-07-18",
end_date: "2025-10-18"
}
})
- Orchestrator executes via Nessie service
- Returns: 43 transactions totaling $1,845 over 3 months

**Step 4: Gemini Processes Result**

- Calculates: $1,845 / 3 = $615/month average
- Calculates 10% reduction: $615 × 0.90 = $553.50/month
- Rounds for usability: $550/month

**Step 5: Second Function Call**

- Gemini calls: create_budget({
user_id: "user123",
category: "Groceries",
amount: 550,
period: "monthly"
})
- Orchestrator executes
- Returns: Budget created successfully with ID budget456

**Step 6: Response Generation**

- Gemini structures response for agent:
    - Main point: "Budget created at $550/month"
    - Context: "This is $65 less than your current $615 average"
    - Validation: "Totally achievable with some meal planning"
    - Encouragement: "That's $780 in savings this year!"
    - Follow-up: "Want tips on cutting grocery costs?"

**Step 7: Agent Delivery**

- Nova receives structured response
- Converts to natural speech with personality
- Delivers to user with warm, encouraging tone

**Function Calling Error Handling:**

**Error Type 1: Function Not Found**

- Gemini calls non-existent function
- Orchestrator returns error: "Function 'get_credit_score' not available"
- Gemini adapts: "I can't check your credit score directly, but I can estimate based on your payment history..."

**Error Type 2: Invalid Parameters**

- Gemini calls with wrong data type
- Orchestrator validates and returns specific error
- Gemini corrects and retries
- If fails 3 times, agent explains limitation

**Error Type 3: External Service Failure**

- Nessie API returns 500 error
- Orchestrator catches and returns: "Temporary service issue"
- Gemini generates fallback response
- Agent explains: "I'm having trouble accessing your account data right now. Can we try again in a moment?"

**Error Type 4: Rate Limiting**

- Too many calls in short period
- Orchestrator queues request
- Agent narrates: "Let me gather all that information... this will take just a moment..."
- Execute calls with delays to stay under limits

### **Response Structure Generation**

**Purpose:** Create blueprint for agent to deliver engaging, helpful responses

**Process:**

**Step 1: Analyze Required Information**

- What facts must be included (balance, amount, date)
- What context enhances understanding (comparison, trend, impact)
- What emotion is appropriate (celebrate, concern, neutral, encourage)

**Step 2: Structure Selection**

- Choose narrative structure:
    - **Direct Answer:** "Your balance is $2,450"
    - **Educational Format:** "Your balance is $2,450. Let me explain what that means for your goals..."

**Step 3: Tone and Personality Guidance**

- Specify emotional tone:
    - Joy: 0.8 (very positive news)
    - Concern: 0.3 (slightly worrying but manageable)
    - Urgency: 0.1 (not time-sensitive)
    - Authority: 0.7 (confident in recommendation)
- Voice modulation instructions:
    - Pitch: Slightly raised (enthusiastic)
    - Pace: Normal (120 wpm)
    - Emphasis words: ["great", "achieved", "proud"]

**Step 4: Content Layering**

- **Layer 1 (Essential):** Direct answer to question
- **Layer 2 (Action):** What to do next
- **Layer 3 (Education):** Learning opportunity

### **Educational Content Generation**

**Purpose:** Create personalized financial lessons from user's real data

**Process:**

**Step 1: Identify Teaching Moment**

- User encounters financial concept (compound interest, APR, diversification)
- User's transaction triggers lesson (first stock purchase → explain dividends)
- User asks "why" question → Deep explanation opportunity
- Milestone achieved → Celebrate with knowledge reinforcement

**Step 2: Assess User's Current Knowledge**

- Check conversation history for related topics
- Review past questions (has user asked about this before?)
- Determine knowledge level: novice, intermediate, advanced
- Adjust complexity accordingly

**Step 3: Create Personalized Example**

- Use user's actual numbers (not generic examples)
- Connect to user's goals
- Show personal impact

**Example: Teaching Compound Interest**

**Generic textbook version:**
"Compound interest means you earn interest on your interest. For example, if you invest $1,000 at 8% annually, after year 1 you have $1,080. Year 2, you earn 8% on $1,080, not just the original $1,000."

**Nova's personalized version (powered by Gemini):**
"Remember that $200 you just put into savings? Over 30 years at 8% return, that $200 becomes $2,011. But here's the magic: that's not just 8% per year—it's compounding. In year 1, you earn $16. In year 10, you earn $37 on that same $200. By year 30, you're earning $149 that year alone. Your money is making money! Now imagine doing that $200 monthly instead of once—that would grow to $244,000. That's your daily coffee budget turning into a quarter million dollars."

**Step 4: Visual Generation**

- Gemini generates chart specifications
- For compound interest: Exponential curve showing balance over time
- Label key milestones: "At year 10: $3,500", "At year 20: $10,000"
- Highlight: "This $7,000 gap between years 10-20 is compound interest working"
